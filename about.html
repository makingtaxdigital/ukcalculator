About this calculator

This is a small, free tool built to answer one specific question: does HMRC's Making Tax Digital rule apply to your income, and if not yet, when will it?

It exists because most self-employed people, sole traders, and landlords — and anyone else this applies to — have never heard of Making Tax Digital, and the guidance that does exist is often written for accountants rather than for someone just trying to get a straight answer.

It was built by one person, using AI tools to help with the research, the writing, and the code, then checked by hand against HMRC's own published rates and thresholds.

This site isn't affiliated with HMRC or the UK government in any way — it's an independent, unofficial tool. For anything specific to your own situation, gov.uk or a qualified accountant is the right place to go.
