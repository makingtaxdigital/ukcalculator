Privacy

Last updated September 2026

What this site collects

Nothing. The numbers you type into the calculator — turnover, costs, property income — are never sent anywhere. Every calculation happens directly in your own browser, on your own device. There's no server storing your figures, no account, and no form submission involved in using the calculator itself.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not currently use cookies or any analytics or tracking scripts. If that ever changes, this page will be updated to say so plainly before it happens.

Affiliate links

This site doesn't currently use any affiliate links. If that changes, this page will be updated first to explain exactly what's linked and how it works, before any link goes live.

Questions

This is a small, independently run site. If anything here is unclear, the best next step is to avoid entering any information you're not comfortable typing into a free tool — nothing here requires you to.
